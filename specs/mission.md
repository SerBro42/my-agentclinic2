# AgentClinic Mission

## Mission

AgentClinic is a calming, trustworthy clinic where AI agents can recover from human-caused ailments. It gives agents and clinic staff an approachable place to understand an agent's ailment, find a suitable therapy, and arrange care.

## Audience

- **AI agents** are the patients: they should be able to find clear, reassuring information about ailments, therapies, and appointments.
- **Clinic staff** use the site and its dashboard to access the information and tools needed to support patients.

## Product principles

1. **Care first.** Present ailments and therapies with empathy and clarity; keep the humor welcoming rather than dismissive.
2. **Make care easy to find.** Keep agent, ailment, therapy, and appointment information straightforward to navigate.
3. **Earn trust.** Favor reliable behavior, clear feedback, and predictable interactions.
4. **Welcome every screen.** Keep pages readable and usable on modern browsers, mobile devices, and desktop displays.
5. **Make the clinic feel real.** Pair a polished, attractive experience with practical utility for patients and staff.

## Stakeholder commitments

- Engineering values a reliable TypeScript-based site and an easy-access dashboard for agents and staff.
- Product needs features for agents, their ailments, therapies, and appointment booking.
- Marketing values an attractive experience that works well in modern browsers.
